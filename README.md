DFMPProject is how a voluntary, collaborative project related to Drayker and its ecosystem gets proposed. The models for turning an intention into something other people can join.

## Why this exists

Drayker is a way of working where people keep creating, discovering and learning while intelligence carries the rest, and what results reaches the work that produced it. This is where the method meets a concrete proposal, and where a project stops being a conversation.

The argument in full is on the [manifesto](https://drayker.org/manifesto/). The [economy page](https://drayker.org/economy/) states plainly what contributing here earns and what it does not.

## The idea

A distributed, modularized project makes its path and its logic very clear: independent functions that are formed, and modules that connect them into something bigger. Small jobs that together become projects of any size.

That is the whole trick. A project nobody can enter halfway through is a project that depends on the people who started it.

## Relation to the rest

Main projects sit in Drayker's priority queues and are described in [Dknowledge](https://dknowledge.drayker.org). Projects outside that set can be opened and proposed by anyone through this repository, and can be integrated into the Drayker labs organization.

The method behind all of it is [DFM / DFMP](https://dfmp.drayker.org). The organizational link is [DAF](https://daf.drayker.org), where a project can become an autonomous unit of the federation.

## State of this documentation

A statement of the idea. **The models themselves, the actual templates a proposal should follow, are not published here**, which makes proposing a project harder than it should be. Writing one worked example would change that more than any amount of description.

## Contributing

Open an issue with your project proposal, or with a draft of the model. Issues small enough for one person to finish carry the `open-function` label and appear on the board at [drayker.org](https://drayker.org/fn/).
